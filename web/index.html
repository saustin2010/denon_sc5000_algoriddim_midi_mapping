Touching a playing track

What the platter does while djay plays the deck. Nudge is djay's pitch bend, like the edge of a CDJ jog wheel, so a touch never grabs the track. A paused track always scratches, and SHIFT + platter always seeks.

Who decides you let go

With the motor off the platter spins free, so a push, a brush or a throw leaves it coasting for seconds. On: once only momentum is turning it, the track plays on and the coast is ignored until your hand moves it again. Off: the track follows the platter until it stops.

djay's acceleration curve: slow moves stay 1:1, fast throws travel further. Off is strictly linear, so every move goes exactly as far as your hand.

Platter ticks per step sent to djay. 2.4 is 1:1 vinyl: one turn of the platter is one turn of djay's on-screen platter. Higher is slower.

How long the platter has to be still before the track plays on. The deck has no touch sensor, so this stillness is the release. Raise it if holding the platter mid-scratch drops out; lower it for a snappier pickup.

Movement reports needed within 100 ms before a scratch starts. Filters the stray ticks a settling platter gives off. Lower reacts to the smallest touch; higher ignores more bumps.

How much platter one tempo-nudge step takes on a playing track. Higher is gentler.

How often the platter is passed on. The deck reports about 1,000 times a second, far more than djay's own SC5000 mapping sends, but djay keeps up on this Mac, so leave it at every report. It's here for a slower machine. Steps are combined, not dropped, so the platter still travels exactly as far.

Trying djay (no-touch) or turning the throw boost off for the first time? Those use platter bindings added to the mapping today. In djay, go to Preferences → Devices → SC5000M Proxy, switch the mapping away and back once so djay reads the new file. The default settings work either way.

Motor

The Vinyl button then switches motor mode, and its light shows when it's on: the platter spins at 33⅓ while the deck plays, like a turntable. Hold it to stop the track, scratch while you hold it, and let go to have the motor run it back up. The pitch-bend buttons work as normal throughout.
